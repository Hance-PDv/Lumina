Privacy Policy — Lumina

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Lumina is an online music application for Android, developed by Hance. This policy describes how the application handles information, device permissions, and internet connectivity.

1. Information & Permissions

Lumina does not collect or store personally identifiable information. No account registration or personal details are required to access the application's core features.

The application may request access to your device's audio files solely to access, organize, display, and play your local music library.

2. Internet Connectivity

Lumina uses an internet connection to access its online music library, stream supported tracks, retrieve music information, and provide the option to request songs for potential inclusion in the library.

Internet connectivity is not intentionally used to transmit error reports, diagnostic data, usage analytics, or personal information.

Your local music files and personal music library are never uploaded to external servers.

3. Local Data Storage

Your playlists, favorites, listening history, playback statistics, settings, and preferences are processed and stored locally on your device, depending on the features available in the application.

This information remains under your control and is not synchronized with external servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Lumina does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

The application does not include advertising networks or third-party tracking services.

5. Data Retention & Control

Application data is retained locally until you clear Lumina's data or uninstall the application. These actions may permanently remove locally stored playlists, settings, and preferences.

Song requests submitted through the application are used to review requested music and consider it for inclusion in the music library. Such requests may be retained for this purpose.

6. Children's Privacy

Lumina does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in Lumina's features or privacy practices. Any revisions will be published with an updated effective date.
