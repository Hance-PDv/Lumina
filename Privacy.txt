Privacy Policy — Nox Sound

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Nox Sound is an Android music application developed by Hance. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information, permissions, local data, and internet connectivity.

1. Information & Permissions

Nox Sound does not require account registration for its core features and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information.

The application may request access to audio files on your device solely to scan, organize, display, and play your local music library.

2. Google Sign-In

Nox Sound provides Google sign-in through Google's official authentication page.

Login credentials are entered directly through Google's official interface and are not received, accessed, or stored by the developer. Nox Sound does not have access to your Google password.

3. Internet Connectivity

Nox Sound uses an internet connection to provide online music discovery, streaming, retrieve music information, and support downloading functionality.

Network requests may expose standard technical information required for communication, such as your IP address and request information.

Nox Sound does not intentionally transmit personal information, usage analytics, or diagnostic reports to the developer.

4. Local Data Storage

Playlists, favorites, listening history, playback statistics, downloaded files, settings, and other preferences may be stored locally on your device, depending on the features you use.

This data is not synchronized with the developer's servers.

5. Data Sharing

Nox Sound does not sell, rent, or intentionally share users' personal information for advertising or commercial purposes.

The application does not include advertising networks or intentionally implemented user-tracking systems.

6. Data Retention & User Control

Locally stored application data remains on your device until you remove it, clear the application's data, or uninstall Nox Sound.

These actions may permanently delete playlists, favorites, listening history, settings, downloaded files, and other locally stored information.

Music requests submitted through the application may be retained and reviewed for music availability and potential library inclusion.

7. Children's Privacy

Nox Sound does not intentionally collect personal information from children.
